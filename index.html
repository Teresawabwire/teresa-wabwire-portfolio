Professional Profile

Executive Profile

I am a business development, structured finance and risk management executive with over 18 years of experience building commercial portfolios, originating and structuring financing transactions, strengthening credit governance and developing strategic partnerships across Kenya's banking, fintech, SME finance and agribusiness sectors.

My experience spans corporate and SME banking, trade finance, invoice discounting, factoring, supply chain finance and impact lending. I have built and managed business portfolios, led commercial and credit functions, structured working capital solutions, mobilised investment capital and developed risk-sharing frameworks involving development finance institutions, guarantee providers, insurers and strategic commercial partners.

I bring a combination of commercial leadership and credit expertise, with a practical understanding of how capital moves through businesses and value chains. My work has included unlocking financing for SME suppliers, supporting agricultural exporters, developing financing models for underserved enterprises and establishing partnerships that connect businesses to markets, capital and institutional support.

My leadership approach is grounded in commercial discipline, sound risk judgement, strategic execution and measurable outcomes. I am particularly interested in opportunities that bring together enterprise growth, investment mobilisation, financial innovation and inclusive economic development.

As Founder and CEO of Amanzi Africa, I am also advancing solutions that address information and financing gaps affecting agricultural enterprises and underserved producers through technology-enabled market intelligence and enterprise support.
