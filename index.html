Professional Profile

Executive Profile

I am a business development, structured finance and risk management executive with over 18 years of experience building commercial portfolios, originating and structuring financing transactions, strengthening credit governance and developing strategic partnerships across Kenya's banking, fintech, SME finance and agribusiness sectors.

Read the full executive profile

My experience spans corporate and SME banking, trade finance, invoice discounting, factoring, supply chain finance and impact lending. I have built and managed business portfolios, led commercial and credit functions, structured working capital solutions and developed partnerships involving development finance institutions, guarantee providers, insurers and strategic commercial partners.

I bring a combination of commercial leadership and credit expertise, with a practical understanding of how capital moves through businesses and value chains. My work has included supporting SME suppliers, agricultural exporters and underserved enterprises to connect with markets, capital and institutional support.

My leadership approach is grounded in commercial discipline, sound risk judgement, strategic execution and measurable outcomes. I am particularly interested in opportunities that bring together enterprise growth, investment mobilisation, financial innovation and inclusive economic development.

Entrepreneurship & Innovation

Amanzi Africa

Founder & CEO

Alongside my professional work in SME finance and risk, I am building Amanzi Africa to address information and financing gaps affecting agricultural enterprises and underserved producers.

Through HELA, a technology-enabled producer intelligence and risk-visibility initiative in development, the ambition is to make enterprise activity more visible to lenders and value-chain partners, strengthen decision-useful information and help connect viable businesses with appropriate capital and risk-sharing support.

This work brings together my experience in credit, value-chain finance, partnerships and inclusive enterprise development.

The HELA proposition

HELA is being developed as a technology-enabled producer intelligence and risk-visibility initiative. Its ambition is to organise useful enterprise information, strengthen the quality of information available for decisions and help connect viable businesses with appropriate capital and risk-sharing support.

Producer visibilityStructured enterprise profiles and more reliable producer information.
Risk intelligenceUseful insight into enterprise activity, value chains and financing readiness.
Capital connectivityStronger links among enterprises, financiers and value-chain partners.

The broader ambition is to support better-informed financing and partnership decisions across agricultural value chains.
