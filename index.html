Professional Profile

About Me

I am a business development, credit and risk management professional with over 18 years of experience spanning banking, SME finance, fintech, working capital solutions, agribusiness finance and development finance.

My professional focus is at the intersection of capital, enterprise growth and risk management. I bring experience in building business relationships, originating and structuring financing opportunities, assessing credit risk, managing portfolios and developing strategic partnerships that expand access to finance.

I am particularly interested in innovative financing models that address the capital constraints facing SMEs, agricultural enterprises and businesses operating within underserved markets.

My approach combines commercial discipline, sound credit judgement, institutional relationship management and a strong understanding of the realities of emerging markets.
